🎯 Job Scorer setup

A few questions and you're done. You'll need an OpenAI API key; email is optional.

1 OpenAI key

The tool uses OpenAI to score jobs. Get a key at platform.openai.com/api-keys. It stays on your computer.

Reasoning is automatically turned off for reasoning models (o-series / GPT-5) to keep scoring fast and cheap.

2 Where do you want to work?

Type a city or region and pick it from the list — or use the Switzerland quick-add below. Add as many as you like.

3 What are you looking for?

Add the fields/roles you want, your target seniority, and the languages you speak. Press Enter to add each one.

4 Your CV

Choose a PDF, .txt, or .md CV. It's matched against each job.

5 Email alerts optional

Get an email when good jobs are found. Skip this if you'd rather just check the dashboard.

6 How often to check

Fewer = faster & cheaper. 50 is a good start.

Out of 10. Higher = only the strongest matches get emailed.
